# User Stories by Role

## 🧑‍🔧 Crew Member

**Inventory & Loadouts**
- Scan a single loadout QR code so the whole kit checks out at once instead of item by item.
- See what's still missing from the template while loading the truck, so nothing gets left behind.
- Flag damage with one tap and a photo on return, so disputes are settled with evidence, not memory.
- Text a photo of a receipt when buying something and have it logged as an expense *and* added to inventory in one step.
- Update a consumable's on-hand count directly from the field ("used the last of the stone dust," "picked up 500sqft of sod for Site 7").

**Ordering**
- Text or voice-note a material request ("need blades and 3 bags of sand for Site 7 tomorrow") without opening an app.
- Get the agent's understanding echoed back before it executes, so a misheard voice note doesn't send the wrong thing to the wrong site.
- Get told about a substitution instead of a flat "unavailable," to make a call on the spot.

**Scheduling & Check-in**
- Receive shift assignment and loadout by WhatsApp the night before.
- Confirm or decline a shift with a single reply, so a no-show is known before the day starts.
- Have arrival/break/departure auto-detected by geofence instead of manual check-in texts.
- Text "SOS" to skip all normal flow and immediately alert management with location.
- See where the rest of the crew currently is and their status, instead of asking "where are you" in the group.
- Get notified when a teammate's status changes (arrived, delayed, redirected).

**Vehicles**
- Text "taking truck 3 to the dump" so the trip gets labeled correctly without manual mileage logging.

**Documents**
- Text a photo of a weigh ticket or receipt and have it filed automatically under the right site.
- Ask "send me the permit for Site 7" and get the file back in chat.

---

## 👷 Crew Lead

- Set up tomorrow's loadout by texting the agent the night before.
- Be warned about conflicts ("compactor already assigned elsewhere tomorrow") during preplanning.
- Claim an open shift broadcast to the crew pool.
- Send a daily safety-talk checklist by WhatsApp that the crew confirms before starting.
- Ask "what's currently at Access Storage" and get a real answer.
- Report a site issue and get redirected to a new site with an updated loadout diff.

---

## 🏗️ Yard / Warehouse Staff

- Get an auto-generated pick list each morning from all confirmed orders.
- Get alerted on low-stock consumables before they hit zero.
- Register new equipment by scanning a blank QR and filling in one form.
- See overdue equipment (past expected return) as a flagged list.

---

## 🧑‍💼 Management / Owner

**Visibility**
- See a live map of every site, truck, and flagged item at once.
- Ask "who has the plate compactor right now" and get an instant answer.
- Receive a weekly digest of utilization, overdue items, and cost-per-job automatically.

**Scheduling & Assignment**
- Assign crews to sites by texting the agent.
- Get shift confirmations and no-shows automatically, before the day starts.
- Get alerted when a crew is idle on-site with no active task.
- Get alerted when a truck is significantly overdue or arrives at the wrong site.
- Get a stalled order escalated automatically instead of it quietly blocking a job all day.

**Approvals & Spend**
- Set a dollar threshold below which orders auto-fulfill and above which they route for a yes/no.
- Approve a batch of pending orders in one WhatsApp reply.

**Vendors & Procurement**
- Have order details compiled and sent to `info@thesodboys.ca` for office staff to forward to the actual vendor — no direct vendor ordering yet.
- Optionally have order details sent directly to whoever's picking it up.
- Get low-stock auto-reorder alerts, without the system contacting a vendor on its own.
- Have disposal tickets (photographed by crews) OCR'd and logged against the right site automatically.

**Vehicles & Compliance**
- Get real-time crew/vehicle position from WhatsApp shared location — no hardware for the POC.
- Get maintenance reminders based on manually logged mileage/hours for now.
- Have a written policy covering what's tracked and why before location/time monitoring goes live (Ontario employee-monitoring disclosure requirements).

**Documents**
- Have every contract, permit, photo, and receipt for a job filed under one job record.
- Get expiry alerts for insurance, certifications, and permits.
- Ask "show me everything for Site 7" and get the full document set back.
